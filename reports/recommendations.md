# 🎯 Strategic Business Recommendations & Retention Action Plan

**Project:** Olist E-Commerce Retention & Churn Reduction  
**Audience:** Chief Commercial Officer (CCO), VP of Operations, Marketing Leadership  

---

## 1. Executive Problem Statement
Olist's business model currently operates as a "leaky bucket":
1. **Low Baseline Retention:** Only 3.00% of customers return, forcing marketing to perpetually spend on customer acquisition.
2. **Immediate First-Purchase Cliff:** 99.4% of buyers do not return in Month 1.
3. **Logistics Erosion:** 8.11% of deliveries arrive past the promised date, triggering a 40.2% drop in review scores and a 10x surge in 1-star reviews.

### Campaign 1: VIP Win-Back Blitz (Target: "At Risk" Segment)
* **Audience:** 553 customers with past repeat purchase history who haven't ordered in ~300+ days.
* **Mechanism:**
  * Automated email/SMS sequence offering **Free Shipping + 15% discount code** valid for 14 days.
  * Personalized recommendations featuring complementary categories to their past purchases.
* **Goal:** Reactivate 15% of the segment (~80 customers) generating ~R$ 24,000 in immediate gross bookings.

### Campaign 2: The 21-Day Post-Delivery Second Order Trigger (Target: "Potential Loyalists")
* **Audience:** 14,454 high-value single buyers (AOV R$ 302.03) within 90 days of order.
* **Mechanism:**
  * Schedule an automated email **exactly 14 to 21 days after delivery confirmation**.
  * Dynamic cross-sell bundle: "Customers who bought [Product Category] also loved these related accessories."
  * Time-limited loyalty credit: "You have R$ 25 in store credit expiring in 7 days."
* **Goal:** Double second-month conversion from 0.45% to 2.5% in this segment, adding ~300 orders/month.

### Campaign 3: Champions & Brand Ambassador Club (Target: "Champions")
* **Audience:** 1,209 multi-order buyers with 4.28 average rating and R$ 322 average spend.
* **Mechanism:**
  * Zero-cost VIP status badge.
  * Priority seller fulfillment and express carrier dispatch.
  * Exclusive early-access access to Black Friday and seasonal mega-sales.
* **Goal:** Increase annual order frequency from 2.15 to 3.0 orders/year.

## 3. Operational & Logistics Remediation Plan

Because late deliveries trigger a collapse in customer reviews (from 4.29 to 2.57 stars), retention cannot be solved by marketing alone.

### 3.1 Proactive Delay Compensation Protocol
* **Current State:** Customers wait in the dark until the estimated delivery date passes, leading to anger and 1-star reviews.
* **Proposed Action:** If tracking telemetry shows an in-transit parcel will breach the SLA by >48 hours:
  * Trigger an automated SMS: *"Your package is slightly delayed in transit. To apologize, we've automatically credited R$ 20 to your Olist wallet for your next purchase."*
* **Anticipated Impact:** Re-frames an operational failure as a proactive customer service touchpoint, neutralizing churn motivation.

### 3.2 Carrier & Regional Seller SLA Enforcement
* Audit seller fulfillment lead times in high-delay states (Alagoas, Maranhão, Rio de Janeiro).
* Introduce a seller fulfillment scorecard: sellers taking >48 hours to hand packages to carrier hubs face lower product ranking algorithms.
